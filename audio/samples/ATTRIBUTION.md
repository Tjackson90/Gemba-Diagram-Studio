# Recorded instruments

Samples from **tonejs-instruments**, edited and assembled by Nicholaus P.
Brosowsky and contributors, licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0**.

Source: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Original sources credited by that collection:

- Acoustic guitar: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios.
- Electric guitar: Karoryfer Samples.
- Nylon guitar: quartertone, Freesound classicalguitar-multisampled, sound 11573.
- Piano, harp and organ: Versilian Studios VSCO Community Edition.

Gemba Studio converted the source MP3 recordings to PCM16 FLAC and trimmed
leading digital silence. The application resamples and processes these recordings
for playback. No endorsement by the original artists is implied.
The nylon sample named D5 upstream is mapped to its measured D#5 pitch.

`manifest.json` records the exact source commit, filenames and SHA-256 checksums
of the bundled files. Original license/source notices are included alongside it.

Suggested credit for shared audio using these recordings: "Instrument samples:
tonejs-instruments / N. P. Brosowsky and original contributors (CC BY 3.0),
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments. Edited and rendered in Gemba Studio."
